Navigation

Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-tap to zoom in, tap a tile for details. To rotate: twist two fingers (touchscreen, or a Mac trackpad in Safari), swipe two fingers sideways on a trackpad, Option/Alt + drag, or drag the compass. Tap the compass to face north.
